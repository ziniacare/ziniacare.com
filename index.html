The ZiniaCare system

Muscle activity.
Made visible.

Muscles produce small electrical signals when they are active. In our research prototype, a textile sleeve, a sensing unit and an app work together to turn those signals into visible feedback.

  1. 01

    A textile sleeve

    Electrodes in the forearm sleeve pick up signals from the muscles involved in wrist and hand movement.

  2. 02

    A sensing unit

    The unit reads the signals from the sleeve and sends them to the app.

  3. 03

    Feedback in the app

    The app displays muscle activity during practice and records training-session information.

Who has taken part in the research?

The published co-design study involved nine clinicians and ten adults living with stroke. They explored the prototype in supervised sessions.

Is the system available to buy or use?

The system is in development and is not available for sale. It is not yet CE marked or FDA cleared. Contact us to discuss the prototype and opportunities for clinical collaboration.

Research informing the design

Shaped by people.
Refined through listening.

In a 2026 co-design study, ten people living with stroke and nine clinicians tried the prototype. Participants valued seeing muscle activity and called for clearer instructions and simpler controls. Their feedback shaped the sleeve and software.

The study explored initial usability in supervised sessions. Long-term home use and rehabilitation outcomes need further evaluation.
